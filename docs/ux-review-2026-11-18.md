# UI/UX review, 2026-11-18

Source-only review by the Vantage reviewer agent (no browser was run, so layout, rendering and screen-reader output are unconfirmed). Items marked *verify* need a check in a real browser. This is a backlog: nothing here has been changed yet. File and line references were accurate at the time of the review.

## Verdict

The visual system is solid and the core contrast is good. The weak points are the sign-in popup and the signed-out experience. The play-money message mostly disappears on phones. The responsible-gambling tools are well thought out, but their explanations come too early and keyboard focus is lost when panels redraw.

**Fix first:** make signed-out blocking opt-in (explicit attribute on real wager and claim buttons) instead of class-based.

## Critical

1. **Signed-out visitors cannot use the public fairness tools, and get the wrong reason.** `src/auth.ts` `ACTIONS` includes `.btn-primary` and `.btn-lg`, so Fairness Verify, the RTP check, the RNG lab and Open chat are blocked with "place bets". Fix: use `data-needs-auth="bet|claim|chat|inventory"` on the real buttons, take the reason from the attribute, keep `debit()` and `grant()` as the second line of defence.
2. **Top-bar Log in button has no accessible name under 560px** (`compliance.css`, `#authBtn span { display:none }`). Add `aria-label`, or keep a short visible label.

## High

3. **Dialog has no name, no focus trap, no focus return** (`src/ui.ts` `modal()`, also `rgui.ts` overlays). Add `aria-labelledby`, trap Tab or set `inert` on the page, restore focus on close.
4. **Form errors are not linked to fields** (`auth.ts`). Add `aria-describedby` and `aria-invalid`, focus the first error, use one live summary instead of many `role="alert"`.
5. **Login errors show below the Google and Steam buttons**, far from Submit. Add a form-level error slot above the button.
6. **Switching tabs wipes typed input; tabs are not real ARIA tabs.** Keep the email across tabs; add `aria-selected`, `tabpanel`, keyboard support.
7. **Password rules appear only after a failed submit; the strength meter is colour-only and uses a length-only score.** Add the "(at least 10 characters)" hint, use `passwordStrength()`, add a text label in a polite live region.
8. *verify* **Cloud start-up may not refresh the visible page** after the async session check (`setSignedIn(false)` runs before `route()`; `home.ts` and `fair.ts` do not redraw on the `state` event). Re-run `route()` or emit an auth event; hide the Log in button until the first check finishes.
9. **Toggling a setting loses keyboard focus** (`account-overview.ts`, `account-rg.ts`, `account-kyc.ts` re-render the whole pane). Update only the toggled element, or restore focus.
10. *verify* **Finish-profile popup can be closed**, leaving a signed-in user with no profile until reload. Make it locked, with a "Not now, sign out" link, or show a persistent banner.

## Medium

11. Play-money message disappears on mobile (`.au-side` hidden under 760px, `.side-foot` hidden under 900px). Add a one-line notice at the top of the popup and a small tag in the mobile top bar. Merge the two duplicate 760px media blocks.
12. New visitors are sent to Log In rather than Register, and are not told what an account gives them. Open Register when the browser has never had an account; reword reasons ("Create a free account to place bets. You get 100 play coins."); add a slim guest bar where the welcome bar would be.
13. Home hero buttons lead signed-out visitors into the guard ("Start a battle", "Open a case"). Offer "Get 100 free coins" (Register) and "Browse cases"; let guests open the battle builder and block only its Create button.
14. Carousel auto-advances with no pause, ignores reduced motion, dots are 9px with no `aria-current`. Add a pause button, pause on focus, skip auto-advance under `prefers-reduced-motion`, label the region, enlarge the hit area.
15. Reduced motion only covers CSS animations, not transitions, the reel spin or JS count-ups. Extend the media query; default FX off when reduced motion is preferred.
16. Toasts are not announced and important messages exist only as toasts. Add `role="status" aria-live="polite"` to `#toasts`; never suppress responsible-gambling, auth or error toasts; replace the "check your inbox" toast with a persistent panel.
17. "Show" password button is small and unlabeled. Add `aria-label`, `aria-controls`, `aria-pressed`; enlarge the hit area.
18. Avatar menu uses `role="menu"` without menu keyboard support; the avatar button has no name; the focus style is a faint tint. Name the button, use disclosure semantics or implement the menu pattern, use a visible outline.
19. No consistent focus style. Add one global `:focus-visible` outline and remove per-component `outline:none`.
20. `--dim` (`#5b6782`) is about 3.1:1 to 3.3:1 on panels and is used as small text (`.nav-group`, `.tickets`, `.slot > span`, `.game-hash`, `.lane-empty`). Use `--muted` for text or lighten `--dim` to about `#7581a0`.
21. *verify* Mobile bottom bar tries to fit 14 links and 3 group headings in one row. Hide group headings, show about 5 items, move the rest into a "More" sheet.
22. Google and Steam buttons show developer text (Supabase README hint) when no backend is connected. Show them disabled with "Not available in this demo", or hide them.
23. On mobile the Register form is long and Google and Steam sit at the bottom. Move them above the email form under 760px; use `92dvh`; do not close a half-filled form on backdrop click.
24. Signed-out Inventory, Race and Rewards pages look broken (zero balance, "Reset demo account", avatar "Y", disabled claims). Render sign-up empty states and previews.
25. The 24-hour loosening rule is explained once, not at the point of change. Show "Tighter: applies now" or "Looser: applies on <date>" while typing; rename "Remove" to "Remove (after 24 h)"; format pending changes with units and a clock time.
26. Cool-off and self-exclusion overlap; a 1-hour self-exclusion is shorter than the shortest cool-off, and the overview calls it "Self lockdown". State the concrete difference, drop or move the 1-hour option, use one name.
27. Account overview hierarchy: the state ("Enabled") is bold and the setting name is small grey; settings are duplicated between the overview and Options; display name editing is at the bottom; an active exclusion uses the success style. Fix the hierarchy, keep settings in one place, show exclusion neutrally.
28. Security tab text is stale: "This demo has no password or email". Rewrite for browser accounts and cloud accounts.

## Low

- Avatar fallback: `onerror="this.remove()"` leaves an empty circle; render the initial underneath the image.
- `.logo-mobile` has no accessible name.
- Popup microcopy: use sentence case; "I have read and agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy"; drop placeholders that repeat labels.
- `.au-hint` has no CSS; the 2FA field is shown to everyone in cloud mode (show it only after the server asks).
- The reality-check "Take a 24 h break" toast should say when the break ends.
- "Set limits now" after the self-assessment only redraws; scroll to and focus the Limits card.
- "Request payout" is a primary button on a play-money site; rename to "Simulate payout" with a quieter style.
- The verification email step asks for an email the account already has; prefill it.
- The welcome toast after local login or register is lost to `location.reload()`; show it after the reload.
- Hero text "Every result is provably fair" overstates the in-browser seed; prefer "Every result can be re-checked".

## Quick wins (under 30 minutes each)

1. `aria-label` on `#authBtn`, `#meBtn`, `.logo-mobile`.
2. `role="status" aria-live="polite"` on `#toasts`.
3. Password hint plus `passwordStrength()` for the meter.
4. `aria-label`, `aria-pressed`, `aria-controls` on Show.
5. Global `:focus-visible` rule; remove `outline:none` on `.me-menu button`.
6. Pause the carousel on focus and under reduced motion; `aria-current` on dots.
7. Keep the typed email when switching tabs.
8. Move login errors above the submit button.
9. `transition-duration` in the reduced-motion query.
10. `--dim` to `--muted` on text labels.
11. Merge the duplicate 760px blocks; add the mobile play-money line.
12. Fix the Security tab text.
13. Open finish-profile as locked.
14. Avatar falls back to the letter.

## Done well, keep

- Core contrast: lime on panels about 15:1; ink on lime buttons about 15.6:1; muted text about 5.7:1 on panels; error red about 5.5:1 (calculated, not measured).
- Real `<label for>` on every field; checkbox lines are fully clickable; the quiz uses `fieldset` and `legend`.
- Honest sign-up: explicit 18+ and Terms boxes, no date-of-birth or jurisdiction theatre; Steam asks only for the two boxes.
- The reason banner in the popup is the right pattern; it needs correct reasons (item 1).
- Responsible-gambling design: tighten now, loosen after 24 h, cancellable; visible pending state; reality check with net result; help links; confirmations for cool-off and self-exclusion.
- Honesty labels: "Demo" tags on simulated feeds, FAQ on simulated players, "Coins have no cash value".
- Escape closes the avatar menu and returns focus; real checkboxes behind the toggles; inactive slides are `visibility:hidden`; the Visual effects toggle (`body.no-fx`).
